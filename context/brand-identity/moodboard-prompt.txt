A vertical brand moodboard for GLOW SOCIETY, a Bangkok wellness social club brand that feels lively, sunlit, social, young-but-not-childish, alive.

LAYOUT — flowing editorial collage:
Organic, magazine-spread composition. 6–9 photographs at different scales that overlap and cascade down the frame, slightly rotated, with soft torn-paper and cut-out edges. One dominant hero photograph occupies the upper third. Smaller supporting images flow around it with generous breathing room. Nothing is aligned to a rigid grid — it should feel hand-arranged on a designer's desk, alive and expressive.

PHOTOGRAPHY — every photo must look like this:
Lifestyle documentary, shot as if by a friend with a good camera. Southeast Asian people aged 22–32, majority women and visibly including queer-presenting people, 2–5 people per frame. Subjects: friends laughing around a stainless steel ice bath, a group talking inside a cedar sauna wrapped in towels, an aerobic dance class mid-movement with arms up, a post-workout group photo with wet hair and towels around necks, a deep red light therapy room. Lighting: bright natural daylight, warm amber inside the sauna, clear cool blue light on the ice bath. Real candid moments, mid-motion, unposed. NOT studio stock photography, NOT posed smiling at camera, NOT dark spa mood with candles or floating flowers.

COLOR — electric cool blue (#0F5FD9) paired with hot orange-red (#FF5A36), accented by a bright glow yellow (#FFD84D), on warm off-white and very pale blue surfaces. The blue-versus-hot pairing is the brand signature: cold plunge against sauna heat.
Colors appear as photo tones, small solid color-chip swatches tucked between images, and one large soft color wash in the background. Keep the palette disciplined — no rainbow, no sage green, no beige spa tones.

TYPOGRAPHY IN THE IMAGE:
One bold headline reading "รีเซ็ตแล้วได้เพื่อนกลับบ้าน" set large in a characterful contemporary Thai display typeface, placed with intention across the composition. Two or three tiny text fragments as texture. Text must be spelled exactly as written and be fully legible.

TEXTURE & DEPTH:
Subtle paper grain over the whole board, soft drop shadows under the photo cut-outs, one or two hand-drawn accent marks (a circle, an arrow, an underline) in the hot orange. Slight vignette.

BOLD CREATIVE CHOICE: one photograph printed as a duotone in electric blue and hot orange, and one image bleeding off the frame edge.

OVERALL: art-directed, premium, unmistakably intentional — a board a real design studio would present to a client. Not a template. Not AI-looking. Not glass morphism. No gradient mesh clichés.

👉 IMAGE ASPECT RATIO: 9:16
👉 ADDITIONAL DETAILS: brand is community-first — every frame should suggest people together, never a solitary person meditating. Two Bangkok locations (Thonglor, Sathorn) so urban context is welcome.
👉 DO NOT: generic stock smiles, western models, fitness-model physiques, watermark, other brand logos, candles, stacked stones, floating flowers.
