# Moodboard Prompt — Flowing / Editorial style

ใช้เมื่อผู้ใช้เลือก **A) Flowing** — เหมาะกับแบรนด์ที่ขายความรู้สึก ความมีชีวิต ความเป็นกลุ่มก้อน
(lifestyle, wellness, community, F&B, creative)

---

## Template

คัดลอกทั้งบล็อกนี้แล้วแทนที่ทุก `[PLACEHOLDER]` — ห้ามเหลือวงเล็บเหลี่ยมในprompt สุดท้าย

```
A vertical brand moodboard for [BRAND_NAME], a [NICHE] brand that feels [BRAND_VIBE].

LAYOUT — flowing editorial collage:
Organic, magazine-spread composition. 6–9 photographs at different scales that overlap and
cascade down the frame, slightly rotated, with soft torn-paper and cut-out edges. One dominant
hero photograph occupies the upper third. Smaller supporting images flow around it with generous
breathing room. Nothing is aligned to a rigid grid — it should feel hand-arranged on a designer's
desk, alive and expressive.

PHOTOGRAPHY — every photo must look like this:
[PHOTOGRAPHY_STYLE]. Lighting: [LIGHTING_MOOD]. Real candid moments, mid-motion, unposed —
people caught laughing, talking, moving. Documentary feel, as if shot by a friend on a good camera.
NOT studio stock photography, NOT posed smiling at camera.

COLOR — [COLOR_DIRECTION]
Colors appear as photo tones, small solid color-chip swatches tucked between images, and one
large soft color wash in the background. Keep the palette disciplined — no rainbow.

TYPOGRAPHY IN THE IMAGE:
One bold headline reading "[PUNCHY_HEADLINE]" set large in a characterful display typeface,
placed with intention across the composition. Two or three tiny text fragments as texture.
Text must be spelled exactly as written and be fully legible.

TEXTURE & DEPTH:
Subtle paper grain over the whole board, soft drop shadows under the photo cut-outs, one or two
hand-drawn accent marks (circle, arrow, underline) in an accent color. Slight vignette.

BOLD CREATIVE CHOICE: [RISK_LEVER]

OVERALL: art-directed, premium, unmistakably intentional — a board a real design studio would
present to a client. Not a template. Not AI-looking. Not glass morphism. No gradient mesh clichés.
```

---

## Notes for filling it

- `[PUNCHY_HEADLINE]` — 2–5 words. Thai is fine (GPT Image 2 renders Thai correctly).
  Make it a **result or a feeling**, not the brand tagline. e.g. "รีเซ็ตแล้วได้เพื่อน"
- `[RISK_LEVER]` — one genuinely bold decision, e.g. "one photograph printed in duotone electric
  blue and hot orange", "hand-written Thai annotations in the margins", "one image bleeding off
  the frame edge"
- If the brand has a logo, describe it in words — do **not** try to reproduce a real logo mark
- Keep the whole prompt under ~2,000 characters; longer prompts dilute the direction
